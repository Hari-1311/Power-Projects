📊 Apple Products Pricing & Competitive Market Analysis Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze 80,000+ Apple product pricing records across major e-commerce platforms, Amazon and Flipkart.

The dashboard provides insights into competitive pricing, discounts, customer engagement, inventory availability, product performance, and promotional events to support data-driven business decisions.

🎯 Business Objectives
The project aims to answer the following key business questions:

Which platform offers better Apple product pricing — Amazon or Flipkart?
Which Apple product categories and models have the highest customer engagement?
Which products have the biggest discounts?
How does Apple product pricing change over time?
Which products are frequently Out of Stock or Low Stock?
Is there a relationship between product ratings, reviews, and prices?
Which sale events provide the best discounts?
Which products should businesses prioritize for inventory and promotions?

🛠️ Tools & Technologies:
Power BI – Dashboard development and visualization
Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
DAX – KPI and business metric calculations
Data Modeling – Organizing data for analytical reporting
Data Visualization – Interactive charts, KPIs, and slicers

💡 Key Insights:
Flipkart showed a slightly lower average Apple product price, while Amazon offered a marginally higher average discount.
iPhone products showed the highest customer engagement based on review volume.
Big Billion Days had the highest observed average discount among the analyzed sale events.
Several highly engaged products also showed significant inventory risk, indicating opportunities for better stock planning.
The analysis identified only a weak relationship between product price, ratings, and reviews, suggesting that multiple factors should be considered when making pricing and promotional decisions.
