# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

> An interactive HR Analytics and Business Intelligence dashboard built with Microsoft Power BI to analyze workforce demographics, employee satisfaction, promotion eligibility, retrenchment status, job roles, service years, overtime, and key HR trends.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to provide meaningful insights into employee data and workforce trends.

The dashboard transforms HR data into interactive visual reports and KPI-driven insights, enabling HR stakeholders to monitor workforce composition, analyze employee satisfaction, identify employees due for promotion or retrenchment, and understand workforce patterns across departments, job roles, service years, and job levels.

The project is designed to support **data-driven HR decision-making** through effective data visualization and interactive dashboard navigation.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- 📈 Analyze overall workforce composition and employee trends.
- 👥 Understand employee demographics and gender distribution.
- 🏢 Analyze employees across departments and job roles.
- ⭐ Monitor employee satisfaction and rating levels.
- ⏱️ Analyze employee overtime patterns.
- 🚀 Identify employees due for promotion.
- ⚠️ Identify employees due for retrenchment.
- 📊 Analyze workforce distribution by job level and service years.
- 🏠 Understand employee distance status.
- 💡 Provide actionable insights for HR decision-making.
